I am a First-Class Computer Science graduate from the **University of Ibadan** (B.Sc., 3.59/4.00) working at the intersection of **multimodal AI, computer vision, and deep learning**. My research focuses on learning from video, audio, and image data together -- from multimodal video summarization to multi-cue person recognition in unstructured environments.

I currently work as a **Backend & Data Engineer at Kole Software**, where I build BigQuery/Vertex AI analytics and ML systems, RAG pipelines, and cloud-native data infrastructure. I am also a **Research Assistant in Computer Vision at the University of Ibadan** and a **Black in AI Emerging Leader in AI (ELAI '26)**.

**Research interests:** Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Multimodal Learning, Transformers, Compression, Visual Recognition, Computer Graphics.

Selected highlights
======
* **Audiosum** -- B.Sc. thesis: a multimodal video summarization framework fusing pre-trained CNN visual features (ResNet50, VGG16) with a custom audio extractor via an FC fusion layer + LSTM temporal modeling. [Manuscript & results](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ifysSjw_GcsJUe7GUZ252RC7v9fhoKe7&usp=drive_fs).
* **Multi-Cue Person Detection & Recognition** -- Research Assistant (Jul--Dec 2025): YOLO posture detection + UNET shadow-signature segmentation + large-scale CVAT video annotation for recognition in unstructured environments.
* **Sales prediction with neural networks** -- LSTM forecasting with sentiment features in a KDD decision-support system; co-authored study on ML-driven inventory optimization (precision/F1 evaluation).
* **Honors:** Black in AI ELAI '26, NHEF Scholar 2024 (Top 100 nationally), Dean's Roll of Honour 2024, BSFUI Certificate of Excellence, MLH Fellow.
